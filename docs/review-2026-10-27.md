## Remaining product limitations

This is still a **frontend demo**: daily template trips, client-side booking
history, demo authentication and sandbox payment. Local inventory checks are not
atomic multi-tab/server reservations and do not synchronize different devices.
Storage-denied fallback lasts only for this page session. Real sales require
server-owned schedules, authenticated bookings, atomic seat holds and payment
verification; this review does not claim those integrations exist or that every
possible defect has been eliminated. Production departure-time/schedule rules
must come from that schedule service.
